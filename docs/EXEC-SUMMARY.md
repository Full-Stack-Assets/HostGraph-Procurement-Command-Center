# Executive Summary — Verified Autonomous Operator

*One-page distillation of [`autonomous-business-ai-plan.md`](./autonomous-business-ai-plan.md).*

## The opportunity

The "autonomous company builder" category (Polsia, NanoCorp, Cofounder, ZeroHuman, Manus,
Genspark) is growing fast but **fragile**: it over-promises full autonomy while frontier
agents complete only ~24–35% of multi-step business tasks on independent benchmarks. The
market leader, Polsia, sits at **2.1/5 on Trustpilot (70% one-star)** — complaints center
on tasks marked "complete" that never deploy, burned credits, unauthorized actions, code
lock-in, and **zero idea validation**. The category's real weakness is **trust, reliability,
and the gap between activity and outcomes**.

## The wedge

Not *more* autonomy — **trust, verification, and a narrow vertical.**

> **"The AI operator that proves its work — and never spends your money on a guess."**

Three structural gaps to exploit: **validation** (no one demand-checks the idea),
**verification** (no one proves the work worked), and **trust/control** (no one gates
irreversible actions). Beachhead: an **autonomous growth operator for B2B micro-SaaS /
indie founders** — validated-first outbound + content + SEO/GEO + ad ops, every action
verified, risky actions approved from your phone.

## How it works — the verified operating loop

`plan → dispatch → verify → gate → govern → report → learn`, every cycle, with proof.
Two of those steps are powered by the **COO Engine** (*Constraint-Optimized Orchestration*):
- **Dispatch** — fitness-scores every (agent, task) pair across five weighted constraints
  (type 30 · cost 25 · latency 20 · load 15 · budget 10) under hard limits.
- **Govern** — predictive spawn + cost-aware retirement keep spend inside hard caps.

The **verification layer** (200 OK + renders, email landed, campaign live within cap,
webhook fired) and **mobile HITL gates** stay independent — the router never grades its own
work. Benchmarked vs. a greedy baseline, the engine showed −23% cost under budget pressure,
+36% throughput in extreme load, −74% SLA misses, and 100% budget compliance (self-reported
simulation — to be re-measured on the real workload).

## Path to first revenue

- **Phase 0 (wk 1–2):** 20 interviews + waitlist; kill/pivot on weak demand.
- **Phase 1 (wk 3–8):** MVP = one verifiable loop; charge the first 10–20 users; ship the public dashboard.
- **Phase 2 (mo 3–4):** nightly cycles, budget governor, export; first 100 customers; instrument churn.
- **Phase 3 (mo 5–9):** vertical playbooks + cross-account learning + SEO/GEO engine.

**Pricing:** flat $49–99/mo, no revenue share, full portability. **Distribution:** build in
public (X + Indie Hackers) + one niche community + "Polsia alternative" SEO/GEO.

## Decision gates

Pivot vertical on weak willingness-to-pay · narrow (don't broaden) if verified task-success
< 60% after Phase 2 · switch channel if no signups by end of Phase 1 · fix retention before
new features if churn > 5%/mo at 50 customers · only broaden scope once public task-success
exceeds 80%.
